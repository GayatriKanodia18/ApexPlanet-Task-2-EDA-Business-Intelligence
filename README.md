# ApexPlanet Task 2 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

This repository contains the work completed for **ApexPlanet Internship – Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Business Intelligence**.

The project focuses on exploring and analyzing a sales dataset to identify important patterns, trends, relationships, and business insights. SQL was used for business-oriented data analysis, while data visualization and dashboarding were used to present the findings clearly.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this task were to:

- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis on the sales dataset.
- Understand the structure and characteristics of the data.
- Analyze sales trends and business performance.
- Identify patterns across categories, cities, genders, products, and customers.
- Perform business analysis using SQL queries.
- Generate SQL query results for business questions.
- Create visualizations and dashboards to communicate insights.
- Prepare a static dashboard mock-up for presentation and submission.
- Document the complete analysis in a structured EDA report.

## Key Insights

The analysis identified several important business insights:

- **Electronics** is one of the strongest-performing sales categories.
- Sales show variation across months, with stronger performance during selected periods.
- **Male customers contribute slightly higher total sales than female customers** in the analyzed dashboard data.
- The **36–45 age group** records the highest sales among the defined age segments.
- The Top 10 customer analysis highlights a concentrated group of high-value customers.
- Product-level analysis helps identify differences in quantity sold and sales contribution.
- City-wise analysis shows variation in sales performance across locations.
